# How to sense current using Ohm's Law (Shunt Resistors)

Shunt resistors are places in circuit, low resistance, low power loss

V = I*R

Rearranged -> I = V/R

Measure Voltage drop -> Amplify and clear noise -> ADC pin of MCU -> Voila

> [!note]
Higher resistance shunt has better signal/noise ratio, higher power loss as well
