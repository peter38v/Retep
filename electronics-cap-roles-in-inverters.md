# Different roles of capacitors in Inverter circuits
2 main types of capactitors in inverters: DC Link & Snubber

## DC Link Cap
Stabilizes bus voltage & supplies peak current to switching bridge

learn more -> [[electronics-dc-link-capacitor]]

## Snub Cap
Absorbs energy from [[parasitic-inductance]] during turn-off, protecting switching
components from voltage spikes.

learn more -> [[electronics-snubber-capacitor]]
