# Calculate for Peak Current


$$
I_g = \frac{\Delta V_{gsDatasheet}}{R_{G,datasheet}+R_{G,int}}
$$


where,


$$
\Delta V_{gsDatasheet} = V_{gs(on)} - V_{gs(off)}
$$
