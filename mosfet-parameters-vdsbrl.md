# Drain-Source Breakdown Voltage for MOSFET

- Denoted by V_ds or V_brdss
- If application voltage > V_ds -> MOSFET cooked
- It can block this voltage without damaging itself
>[!Warning] Good safety margin = 2X Bus Voltage
- Is dependent on [[electronics-junction-temperature|junction-temperature]]
- Also dependent on [[electronics-mosfet-gate-source-voltage|Gate-Source Voltage]]
