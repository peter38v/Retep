# Thermal Resistance
Couple resistances:
- R_jc -> Junction to case resistance -> How many degrees hotter will the outside of the MOSFET
be/Watt of loss
- R_cs -> Case to thermal sink resistance -> How much will the sink go up in temp/Watt of loss
- R_ja -> Junction to Ambient -> How many Celcius will the sorrounding area go up/Watt of loss
>[!note] Junction is the "pad" that the MOSFET sits on
