# R_ds(on)

- RDson is the amount of resistance the mosfet has while conducting/letting current flow
- Higher RDSon value means higher losses and higher heat generation
- But can switch faster

>[!Warning] Low RDson -> usually higher Gate Charge (Qg)
> Lower Qg = Lower weight car -> to get same acceleration (switching freq) -> bigger engine
> (stronger gate driver)--> worse handling though (more parasitics)
