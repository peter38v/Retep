# DC Link Capacitance calculation

For 3 phase inverter:

For Voltage Ripple

$$
C = \frac {I_{ph,pk}}{8 * f * \Delta V}
$$

where, \
$I_{[ph,pk]}$ = Peak Phase Current
$\Delta V$ = Max ripple voltage
f = switching frequency

Lower: frequency or Change in Voltage ups capacitance
Higher: P or K makes Capaciatance go up as well
